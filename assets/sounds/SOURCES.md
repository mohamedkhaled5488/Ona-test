# Sleep section — bundled audio sources

`white-noise.wav`, `pink-noise.wav` and `brown-noise.wav` are generated by
`scripts/generate-noise-assets.js` (30 s mono loops with an equal-power
crossfade at the loop point; deterministic, so re-running reproduces them).
They are math, not recordings — no licensing concerns. The four recordings
below are the only sourced files.

All four were sourced from **Mixkit** (mixkit.co), under the **Mixkit Sound
Effects Free License** — free for commercial and non-commercial use, no
attribution required. License overview: https://mixkit.co/license/
(the specific "Sound Effects Free License" terms are shown in-page behind a
"View License" modal on that page).

| File | Mixkit title | Mixkit item id | Source URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rain.mp3` | Light rain loop | 2393 | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/rain/ |
| `ocean.mp3` | Sea waves loop | 1196 | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/ocean/ |
| `forest.mp3` | Forest birds ambience | 1210 | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/forest/ |
| `fan.mp3` | Stove extractor fan | 1813 | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/discover/fan/ |

Downloaded directly from Mixkit's CDN (`assets.mixkit.co`) on 2026-09-22.
Files are used as-is (not re-trimmed/re-looped in an editor) — expo-audio
loops them with `player.loop` (see `src/utils/audioEngine.ts`), so a very
slight seam may be audible on `forest.mp3` and `fan.mp3` in particular since
they weren't hand-trimmed to a matched waveform loop point. If that's
audible in practice, running each
through Audacity to trim to a clean zero-crossing loop point (per the
implementation guide's Part 2 process) would tighten it further.

**Before shipping**, it's worth a final human click-through of
https://mixkit.co/license/ → "Sound Effects · Free License" to confirm the
terms firsthand — the modal's exact text isn't machine-readable from here,
so this was verified via the site's license-page metadata and Mixkit's
well-documented standard terms rather than the modal copy itself.

No fan-specific "white noise fan" ambience loop was available in Mixkit's
catalog; "Stove extractor fan" was the closest steady fan-drone recording.
