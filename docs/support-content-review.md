# Reflect, Letters and Support: content for review

Generated from `src/content` by `node scripts/content-review.js`. Do not edit by hand.

For the owner (tone) and a clinician or psychologist (safety) before release. ☐ = not yet reviewed. **[heavy]** = about loss, the past, or the heaviest feelings: heavy Support pieces are not shown until reviewed (breathing, grounding and the support lines always are).

**For the clinician, most of all:** the hard-feeling mode lines, the danger card and the phrases that bring it (`src/support/engine.ts`, `DANGER`), every **[heavy]** piece, the Low and Tense zones, the heaviest words (Depressed, Miserable, Helpless, Numb, Ashamed, Insignificant, Rage, Hateful), and the Deep reflection questions. Once a piece is approved, set its `reviewed` to true in its JSON file and run this script again.

Intensity is the feeling sheet's 1 (a little) to 5 (very).

### Tense

**Understand it**

- ☐ Tension often comes with energy your body has made to deal with something: a quicker heartbeat, tight shoulders, busy thoughts. It's usually trying to protect you, even when there's nothing to fight.

#### Lively

**Aware**

- ☐ You're noticing a lot right now. That kind of clear attention is worth enjoying.
- ☐ Mind: **Notice one thing fully**. Pick one thing around you and give it all your attention for a few breaths. _Why: Clear attention can make an ordinary moment feel fuller._ (tool: oneObject)
- ☐ Write: **Write what you see**. What are you noticing today that you usually miss? _Why: Writing down what you notice can help you see it again later._ (write: "What I'm noticing today that I usually miss:")

**Focused**

- ☐ You're in the zone. That's a good place to be.
- ☐ Do: **Protect the focus**. Silence one notification and give the thing in front of you a little longer. _Why: Focus often lasts longer when it has fewer interruptions._
- ☐ Body: **A short stretch break**. When you come up for air, roll your shoulders and look into the distance. _Why: A brief pause can help focus stay comfortable rather than tense._ (tool: stretches)

**Cheerful**

- ☐ Something's making the day lighter. Good.
- ☐ Connect: **Pass it on**. Send someone a cheerful message, just because. _Why: Good moods often grow a little when they are shared._
- ☐ Write: **Note the cause**. What lifted your mood today? _Why: Knowing what cheers you can make it easier to find again._ (write: "What lifted my mood today:")

**Amused**

- ☐ Something made you laugh, or nearly. Hold on to that.
- ☐ Connect: **Share the joke**. Tell someone what made you smile. _Why: Laughing with someone is often even better than laughing alone._
- ☐ Write: **Keep it for later**. Jot down what amused you, for a day that needs it. _Why: A funny moment, written down, can cheer you up again later._ (letter: hardDay)

**Playful**

- ☐ A playful mood. Those are worth making room for.
- ☐ Do: **Play a little**. Doodle, hum, try something silly for a moment. No purpose needed. _Why: Play for its own sake can be a real kind of rest._ (tool: doodle)
- ☐ Connect: **Invite someone in**. Suggest something light to do with someone you like. _Why: Playfulness tends to be catching, in a good way._

**Hopeful**
